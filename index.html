Privacy Policy

CoBible Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 20, 2026
Last Updated: October 20, 2026

Welcome to CoBible. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent approach to data storage, permissions, and third-party integrations.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Data: We do not collect, track, or share any personal identity information. CoBible does not require registration, phone numbers, email addresses, or account creation.

Reading & Study Data: Your bookmarks, reading plan progress, study notes, highlighted verses, and preferences are stored strictly locally on your device (using Hive and SharedPreferences). We have no access to this data, and it is never transferred to any proprietary servers.

2. Third-Party AI Services

CoBible offers generative AI research tools powered by Google Gemini API.

  • When you analyze a Bible chapter or verse, only the selected scriptural excerpt and your specific study prompt are sent to Google Gemini servers to synthesize educational explanations.
  • No personal identifiers, device credentials, or user profiles are sent with these requests.
  • If you specify a custom Google AI Studio key (BYOK), it is retained securely and solely on your device.
  • All interactions are governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Audio Podcasts and Downloads

Bible EPUB files and audio podcasts are streamed or downloaded directly from official, public distribution networks. We do not operate proxy servers that monitor your media consumption.

4. Application Permissions

  • Internet (android.permission.INTERNET): Required to fetch official publication files, stream podcasts, and generate AI insights.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK): Allows continuous audio playback via standard media notifications when the screen is locked.
  • Local Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Optional. Used strictly for on-device daily verse reminders and reading schedules that you configure.

5. Children’s Privacy

CoBible does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13. The application is completely safe for audiences of all ages.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this policy, feel free to contact us via GitHub or through the developer profile link on Google Play.
